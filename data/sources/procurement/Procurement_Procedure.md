# Bluefin Maritime Services - Spare Parts Procurement Procedure

Procedure number: TEC-PRO-021. Revision 5, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to technical superintendents, the purchasing team and fleet managers ordering spares for managed vessels.

## 1. Requisitions

All spare part orders start with a requisition raised by the vessel or the superintendent in the planned maintenance system. A requisition must state the vessel name, equipment, maker's part number or Bluefin catalogue part number, quantity, required-by date and delivery port.

## 2. Approval limits

Purchase orders are approved according to their total value in US dollars, after discounts and before delivery charges:

- Up to USD 10,000: technical superintendent
- Above USD 10,000 up to USD 50,000: fleet manager
- Above USD 50,000: technical director

Splitting an order into smaller purchase orders to stay within an approval limit is not allowed.

## 3. Quotations

For orders above USD 25,000, at least three quotations must be obtained unless the part is available only from the original equipment maker or a framework supplier listed in the catalogue. For catalogue items from framework suppliers (NordMarine and AsiaPac Marine), the catalogue price may be used without further quotations.

## 4. Stock and warehouse priority

Before ordering from a supplier, check Bluefin's own stock. Parts held in Bluefin warehouses are issued first. When the same part is stocked in more than one warehouse, issue from the warehouse closest to the delivery port. A part is considered in stock only if the catalogue stock quantity is equal to or greater than the requested quantity.

## 5. Emergency purchases

An emergency purchase is an order needed to keep a vessel seaworthy or to prevent off-hire. The superintendent may place an emergency order up to USD 25,000 by email before the purchase order is approved, but the purchase order must be raised and approved in the system within 2 working days.

## 6. Critical spares

Items flagged as critical in the catalogue must always be kept on board at the minimum quantity stated in the vessel's critical spares list. When a critical spare is used, a replacement requisition must be raised within 24 hours.

## 7. Receiving and quality

Spares delivered on board must be checked by the chief engineer against the packing list and certificates. Damaged or wrong items must be reported to purchasing within 7 days of delivery so that supplier return windows are not missed.

## 8. Contacts

Purchasing team: purchasing@bluefin-maritime.example. Fleet managers are listed in the vessel management matrix.

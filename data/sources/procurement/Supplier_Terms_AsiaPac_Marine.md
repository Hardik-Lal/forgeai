# AsiaPac Marine Supply Pte Ltd - Supply Conditions for Bluefin Maritime Services

Agreement reference: APMS/BMS/26-02. Effective 1 March 2026 for 24 months. AsiaPac Marine supplies pumps, valves, deck machinery spares, safety equipment and consumables from warehouses in Singapore and Mumbai.

## 1. Pricing

Catalogue prices are in US dollars, ex-warehouse Singapore or Mumbai, excluding GST and delivery. Prices are reviewed every 6 months; revised prices apply only to orders placed after the revision date.

## 2. Quantity discount

A discount of 3% applies to the line value when 20 or more units of the same part number are ordered on one purchase order. There is no order-value discount. The quantity discount does apply to urgent orders.

## 3. Lead time

Catalogue lead times are calendar days to ready-for-dispatch at the stated warehouse. For deliveries to a port outside Singapore and Mumbai, add 5 calendar days for transit to the delivery port.

Urgent orders placed before 12:00 Singapore time are dispatched the same day for in-stock items, for a flat fee of USD 250 per order.

## 4. Delivery

Delivery to any vessel or agent within Singapore port limits is free of charge for orders above USD 2,000. Below USD 2,000 a delivery charge of USD 85 applies. Deliveries in Mumbai are made to Bluefin's Mumbai store at Sewri at no charge. Launch hire for anchorage deliveries is charged at cost.

## 5. Payment

Payment is due 30 days from invoice date. A 2% early-payment discount applies if payment is made within 10 days of the invoice date.

## 6. Warranty

Pumps and valves carry an 18-month warranty from delivery. Deck machinery spares carry a 12-month warranty. Consumables and safety equipment with an expiry date (for example flares and lifejacket lights) are warranted until the printed expiry date only. Warranty claims must be notified within 14 days of discovering the defect, with photographs and the vessel's maintenance record.

## 7. Cancellation and returns

Orders may be cancelled free of charge before dispatch. Once dispatched, a cancellation fee of 10% of the order value applies. Returns of unused standard items are accepted within 21 days of delivery without a restocking fee if the item is in its original sealed packaging.

## 8. Safety equipment

Pyrotechnics and other dangerous goods are shipped only as dangerous goods cargo and require an additional 3 calendar days of lead time. AsiaPac does not service liferafts; liferaft servicing must be booked with an approved service station.

## 9. Contacts

Sales: sales@asiapac-marine.example. Urgent orders hotline: available 24 hours through the sales desk.

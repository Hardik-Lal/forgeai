# NordMarine Spares AS - Commercial Terms of Supply for Bluefin Maritime Services

Framework agreement reference: NMS-BMS-2026-07. Valid from 1 February 2026 to 31 January 2028. NordMarine supplies main engine, auxiliary engine, purifier and compressor spares from its Rotterdam warehouse and its Singapore stockist.

## 1. Prices

Prices in the Bluefin spares catalogue are NordMarine list prices in US dollars, ex-warehouse, excluding VAT/GST, packing for sea freight and delivery. Prices are fixed for the validity of this agreement except for raw-material surcharges announced with at least 60 days of notice.

## 2. Volume discounts

NordMarine grants a discount on the value of each single purchase order (before delivery charges):

- Order value from USD 5,000 up to USD 14,999.99: 5% discount
- Order value of USD 15,000 or more: 8% discount

Discounts are not cumulative across orders and do not apply to urgent deliveries.

## 3. Lead times and urgent delivery

Catalogue lead times are calendar days from order confirmation to ready-for-dispatch at the stated warehouse. Order confirmation is sent within 1 working day of receipt of a purchase order.

Urgent delivery (dispatch within 48 hours for stocked items) is available with a surcharge of 12% of the order value. Urgent orders are not eligible for volume discounts.

## 4. Delivery to vessels

Delivery is DAP (Incoterms 2020) to the ship's agent or a nominated forwarder at the port. Delivery to the vessel itself, launch hire and port charges are arranged by Bluefin's forwarder and are not included. NordMarine will deliver free of charge to Bluefin's Rotterdam consolidation warehouse for orders above USD 3,000.

## 5. Payment

Payment terms are net 45 days from invoice date. A late payment interest of 1% per month applies to overdue invoices.

## 6. Warranty

All new spares carry a warranty of 12 months from installation or 18 months from delivery, whichever ends first. The warranty covers defects in material and workmanship. It does not cover wear parts such as gaskets, O-rings and filter elements, damage from improper installation, or use with fuel or lubricating oil outside the engine maker's specification.

## 7. Returns

Unused items in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery with a restocking fee of 15% of the item price. Items made to order, cut-to-length items and electrical items are non-returnable.

## 8. Certificates and documentation

Each delivery includes a packing list, a certificate of conformity and, for pressure-retaining parts, a material certificate (EN 10204 3.1). Class certificates (DNV, ABS, LR) are available on request at USD 150 per certificate and add 5 working days to the lead time.

# Bluefin Maritime Services - Leave Policy for Shore Staff

Policy number: HR-POL-004. Version 3.2, effective 1 April 2026. Applies to all permanent shore-based employees in the Mumbai and Singapore offices. Seafarers on articles are covered by the separate Crew Leave and Relief Policy and are out of scope of this document.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the types of leave available to shore staff, how leave is earned, how it is applied for and approved, and what happens to unused leave. Where local law in India or Singapore provides a more generous entitlement, the statutory entitlement applies.

## 2. Leave year

The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Entitlements are pro-rated for employees who join or leave during the leave year, rounded to the nearest half day.

## 3. Annual leave

Permanent shore staff are entitled to 24 working days of paid annual leave per leave year. Annual leave accrues at 2 working days per completed month of service.

Employees on probation accrue annual leave from their first day but may not take annual leave during the first 3 months of probation, except with written approval from the Head of HR in exceptional circumstances.

Annual leave of more than 3 consecutive working days must be applied for in the HRMS portal at least 14 calendar days in advance. Shorter absences must be applied for at least 2 working days in advance. Line managers must approve or decline a request within 3 working days of submission.

## 4. Carry forward and encashment

Up to 10 days of unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next leave year. Carried-forward leave must be used by 30 June of the new leave year; any carried-forward days not used by 30 June lapse automatically.

Leave cannot be encashed while in service. On resignation or termination, unused accrued annual leave of up to 15 days is paid out at the basic daily salary rate. Days above 15 lapse.

## 5. Sick leave

Shore staff are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per leave year. Sick leave does not carry forward and cannot be encashed.

A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days, and for any sick leave taken on the working day immediately before or after a public holiday or approved annual leave. Employees must inform their line manager before 10:00 local time on the first day of absence.

In Singapore, hospitalisation leave follows the Employment Act: up to 60 days per year inclusive of the 12 days of paid sick leave, where hospitalisation is certified by a doctor.

## 6. Parental leave

Maternity leave: female employees with at least 80 days of service in the 12 months before the expected date of delivery are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave in India. In Singapore, eligible employees receive 16 weeks of paid maternity leave in line with the Government-Paid Maternity Leave scheme.

Paternity leave: male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption of the child.

Adoption leave: employees adopting a child below 12 months of age are entitled to 12 weeks of paid adoption leave.

## 7. Bereavement and compassionate leave

Employees may take up to 5 working days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law). Up to 2 working days apply for extended family members. Bereavement leave does not need advance application but must be recorded in HRMS within 5 working days of return.

## 8. Marriage leave

Employees are entitled to 3 working days of paid marriage leave once during their employment with the company, to be taken within 1 month of the date of marriage.

## 9. Leave without pay

Leave without pay (LWP) of up to 30 calendar days may be granted when all paid annual leave has been exhausted, with approval from both the line manager and the Head of HR. LWP beyond 30 days requires approval from the Country Manager. Periods of LWP do not count towards leave accrual.

## 10. Public holidays

Each office publishes its list of public holidays in December for the following calendar year. Mumbai observes 12 public holidays per year and Singapore observes the 11 gazetted public holidays. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive one day of compensatory off, to be used within 60 days.

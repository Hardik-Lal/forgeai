# Bluefin Maritime Services - Hybrid Working and Code of Conduct

Policy number: HR-POL-009. Version 1.4, effective 1 April 2026. Applies to permanent shore staff in the Mumbai and Singapore offices.

## Part A - Hybrid working

### A1. Eligibility

Employees who have completed probation may work from home for up to 2 days per week, subject to their role. Roles that require physical presence, such as reception, the document control room and the 24x7 fleet operations desk, are not eligible for regular work from home. Employees on probation work from the office full time.

### A2. Arrangement and approval

Work-from-home days are agreed with the line manager in advance and recorded in the HRMS attendance calendar by Friday of the preceding week. Line managers may ask employees to attend the office on a planned work-from-home day for business reasons, giving at least 24 hours of notice.

### A3. Core hours

All employees, whether in the office or at home, must be available between 10:00 and 16:00 local time on working days. Outside core hours, flexible start and finish times are allowed as long as the contracted 40 hours per week are worked.

### A4. Equipment and allowances

The company provides a laptop, headset and, on request, one external monitor. Employees approved for hybrid working receive a one-time home office set-up allowance of INR 15,000 in India or SGD 400 in Singapore, claimable once every 3 years. A monthly internet allowance of INR 1,000 or SGD 30 is paid through payroll to employees on a hybrid arrangement.

### A5. Working from another location

Working from a location other than the registered home address within the same country is allowed with line manager approval. Working from another country is allowed for at most 15 working days per calendar year and requires approval from the line manager and HR at least 10 working days in advance, because of tax and immigration implications.

### A6. Information security at home

Company data may only be accessed through the company laptop and VPN. Printing confidential documents at home is not allowed. Video calls involving client, vessel or crew personal data must be taken where others cannot overhear. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 2 hours.

## Part B - Code of conduct

### B1. Respect at work

Bluefin has zero tolerance for harassment, bullying and discrimination on the basis of gender, race, religion, caste, nationality, age, disability or sexual orientation. Complaints of sexual harassment in India are handled by the Internal Committee under the POSH Act; in Singapore by the HR Business Partner and Country Manager.

### B2. Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from suppliers, shipyards, agents or clients only up to a value of USD 50 per occasion and USD 150 per year from the same party. Anything above these limits must be declined or declared to Compliance within 5 working days using the Gifts Register. Cash or cash equivalents, such as vouchers, must never be accepted.

### B3. Conflicts of interest

Employees must declare any personal or family interest in a supplier, agent, shipyard or competitor to Compliance before taking part in any decision involving that party, and must update the declaration every year in April.

### B4. Confidentiality and data protection

Crew personal data, vessel commercial terms and client information are confidential. They may only be shared with colleagues who need them for their work and with external parties under a contract. Personal data is processed in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and Singapore's PDPA.

### B5. Social media

Employees must not post photographs taken on board vessels, details of incidents, or any client information on social media. Only Corporate Communications may comment publicly on behalf of the company.

### B6. Speaking up

Concerns about fraud, bribery, safety violations or breaches of this code can be raised with the line manager, HR, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at ethics@bluefin-maritime.example. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary offence.

### B7. Breaches

Breaches of this code may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. Disciplinary procedures are handled by HR under the Disciplinary Policy (HR-POL-012), which is not part of this document.

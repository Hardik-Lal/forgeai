# Bluefin Maritime Services - Business Travel and Expense Policy

Policy number: FIN-POL-011. Version 2.0, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to shore staff in Mumbai and Singapore travelling on company business, including vessel attendance, dry-dock supervision, client meetings and training.

## 1. Principles

Employees should spend company money as carefully as they would their own. Only reasonable expenses incurred wholly for business purposes are reimbursed. Personal expenses are never reimbursable, even if incurred during a business trip.

## 2. Travel approval

All business travel must be approved in advance in the Travel Request module of HRMS. Domestic travel needs line manager approval. International travel needs line manager and department head approval. Travel to attend a vessel in dry dock must also be approved by the Technical Director.

Travel requests should be raised at least 7 days before departure for domestic trips and at least 14 days before departure for international trips, except for emergency vessel attendance.

## 3. Class of travel

Air travel is economy class for all employees on flights of less than 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or more, employees in grade M3 and above may travel in premium economy. Business class is only permitted for the Managing Director and for flights of more than 10 hours with Country Manager approval.

All flights must be booked through the company's travel desk. Tickets booked directly by employees are reimbursed only if the travel desk could not book within the required time and the fare is not higher than the travel desk quote.

Train travel in India is reimbursed at AC 2-tier class. Taxis and ride-hailing services are reimbursed for business journeys; personal car use is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre in India and SGD 0.60 per kilometre in Singapore.

## 4. Hotels

Hotel bookings should be made through the travel desk within the following nightly caps (room rate excluding taxes):

- Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai and other Indian metros: INR 9,000 per night
- Other Indian cities and port towns: INR 6,000 per night
- Singapore: SGD 280 per night
- Rotterdam, Hamburg and other European ports: EUR 190 per night
- All other international locations: USD 200 per night

Stays above the cap require prior written approval from the department head.

## 5. Per diem

A daily allowance (per diem) covers meals, local incidentals and laundry. It is paid per calendar day away from the home city:

- Domestic travel in India: INR 2,500 per day
- Travel to Singapore: SGD 90 per day
- Travel to Europe: EUR 75 per day
- Travel to all other international locations: USD 70 per day

When meals are provided by a client, a shipyard or the vessel, the per diem is reduced by 30% for each day on which lunch and dinner are both provided. On the day of departure and the day of return, 50% of the per diem is paid if the trip lasts more than one day.

## 6. Travel advance

Employees may request a cash or card travel advance of up to 75% of the estimated trip cost. Advances must be settled in the expense claim for that trip. A new advance is not issued while a previous advance remains unsettled for more than 30 days.

## 7. Submitting expense claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the Expenses module of HRMS within 30 days of returning from the trip. Original or digital receipts are required for every item above INR 500 or SGD 10. Claims for items without a required receipt are reimbursed only with a signed missing-receipt declaration, limited to two declarations per year.

Claims submitted between 31 and 60 days after return need department head approval. Claims submitted more than 60 days after return are rejected.

Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll if approved by the 20th of the month.

## 8. Non-reimbursable expenses

The following are never reimbursed: alcoholic drinks, minibar items, traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment, spa and gym charges, upgrades not approved in advance, travel insurance bought separately (the company group policy already covers business travel), and expenses for family members or companions.

## 9. Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times a year may apply for a corporate card. Corporate card statements must be reconciled in HRMS within 15 days of the statement date. Personal use of the corporate card is a disciplinary matter.
